How it works

Simple steps, real impact

01

List your surplus

Donors post available food with item, quantity, precise address, and expiry date. Takes under two minutes.

02

Browse and request

Receivers browse available donations nearby and submit a pickup request. Donor approves directly.

03

Rider delivers

A rider claims the delivery, picks up from the donor's address, and drops it off at the receiver's door.
